Who are they?

$uicideboy$ is a massively successful American underground hip-hop duo formed in 2013 by cousins Scott "$crim" Arceneaux Jr. and Aristos "Ruby da Cherry" Petrou. Hailing from New Orleans, Louisiana, they are widely recognized as trailblazers of the SoundCloud rap era, blending abrasive trap beats with punk-rock intensity.

$crim

Scrim, whose real name is Scott Arceneaux Jr., is a rapper, producer, and one half of the New Orleans hip-hop duo $uicideboy$. Alongside his cousin Ruby da Cherry, he became known for dark, emotional music that often talks about addiction, depression, personal struggles, and recovery. Scrim also produces much of their music under the name Budd Dwyer and has released solo projects of his own.

RubyDaCherry

Ruby da Cherry, whose real name is Aristos Petrou, is a rapper and one half of the New Orleans hip-hop duo $uicideboy$. Alongside his cousin Scrim, Ruby is known for his fast flows, unique voice, and emotional lyrics. His music often focuses on mental health, personal struggles, relationships, and dealing with fame.
